## Unreleased

- A scene editor on the page: make a scene or change one, preview it on a controller's panels or on a wall of triangles you lay out, try it on the real panels without saving, and keep it in a library beside the backups
- A firmware update, triggered from the command line or the page, is watched through: backed up first, then quiet while it installs, then what came back against what was there

## v0.1.0 (2026-10-06)

First release. The original Nanoleaf Light Panels, without the app.

- Find your controllers and connect to each with one press of its button
- See every scene on every controller side by side, and copy one to the controllers that lost it
- Back up every scene, and put them back on any controller
- Start, rename and delete scenes; paint each panel a colour
- Power, brightness, colour, white, orientation and the Rhythm module's source
- Firmware: see whether an update is waiting, and have the controller install it
- A web page that shows every controller's panels moving as their scene does, with the everyday controls
- Nothing on a controller is replaced unless you say so, and it is backed up first
- A Go library for the whole API, for your own programs
